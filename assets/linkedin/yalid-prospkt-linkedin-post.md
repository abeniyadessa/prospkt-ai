I have been heads-down building at YALID, and I am excited to share a look at Prospkt.ai.

Prospkt is an autonomous AI sales rep for local service businesses. The goal is simple: help owners turn missed follow-ups, cold local opportunities, and messy lead lists into booked jobs without losing control of the customer experience.

What we have been building:

- A lead engine for finding and scoring local businesses
- Campaign lanes for warm recovery, cold B2B outreach, and guarded consumer workflows
- AI calling flows that qualify, follow up, and book appointments
- CRM memory so every call, note, status, and next step is tracked
- Guardrails for budgets, calling windows, opt-outs, DNC handling, and AI disclosure
- A voice demo flow so operators can test the rep before campaigns go live

This has been a fun mix of product, engineering, compliance, voice UX, and practical sales ops. Still early, but the shape is coming together: an AI teammate that handles the repetitive follow-up work while the business owner stays in the driver seat.
