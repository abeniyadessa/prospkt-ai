# LinkedIn Experience Description

## Founder / Product Builder

YALID - Prospkt

Building Prospkt, an AI sales rep and receptionist platform for service-based businesses. Prospkt helps owners recover missed revenue, follow up on estimates, call warm and cold opportunities, book jobs, and keep every action logged in a built-in CRM.

My work spans product strategy, UI/UX, full-stack engineering, AI voice workflows, CRM architecture, campaign automation, onboarding, compliance guardrails, and go-to-market positioning.

Key focus areas:
- Built the product direction around service businesses, missed revenue recovery, estimate follow-up, outbound campaigns, bookings, and CRM memory.
- Designed and developed the web app experience across the command center, campaigns, CRM, pipeline, calls, bookings, settings, onboarding, and voice demo flows.
- Implemented guarded AI sales workflows with daily call caps, budget limits, local calling windows, DNC checks, opt-out handling, and activity logging.
- Integrated core startup infrastructure including Next.js, TypeScript, Clerk, Postgres/Drizzle, Railway, Vapi, OpenAI Realtime, Twilio, Cal.com, and shadcn/ui.
- Built the marketing system, brand assets, LinkedIn creative, and product messaging around the promise of turning service leads into booked jobs automatically.

Prospkt is focused on becoming the revenue layer for service-based businesses: not a field-service operations clone, but the AI rep that finds, calls, follows up, books, and logs opportunities while the owner stays in control.
